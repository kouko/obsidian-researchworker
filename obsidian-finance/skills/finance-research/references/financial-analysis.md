# Financial analysis

Apply the shared evidence and time rules in [research-method.md](research-method.md).

## Research questions

How did profitability, cash generation, working capital and financing change?
Which changes reflect the underlying business, and which reflect accounting,
timing or financing?

## Evidence

Read the original income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement and
relevant notes. Use management discussion and reconciliations for explanations.
Check accruals, debt maturities, liquidity, commitments and contingencies where
material. Retain exact definitions, units, duration starts and original or
restated versions.

## Analysis

Reconcile earnings, cash flow and balance-sheet changes. Compare equivalent
fiscal periods and distinguish quarterly, year-to-date and annual values. A Q4
acquisition may select an annual FY endpoint. Derive standalone Q4 only for
additive flows from compatible periods, with both source operands and an
explicit reconciliation. Never subtract EPS or average shares.

Assess margins, cash conversion, leverage and working capital with the
denominators and adjustments shown. For banks, inspect funding, credit losses
and regulatory capital. Industrial debt, enterprise-value and CFO-minus-capex
shortcuts can misstate their economics. Explain one-offs, scope changes and
periods or metrics that cannot be compared.

## Completion

Record reconciled metrics, material drivers and counterevidence, and link all
operands to their original eligible sources. Leave unsupported metrics or
required reconciliations pending. Pass the supported results and limitations
to dependent valuation or synthesis when applicable.
