# Industry and competition

Apply the shared evidence and time rules in [research-method.md](research-method.md).

## Research questions

What changed in the conditions shared by competitors? Which changes come from
the company itself, and what evidence bears on the persistence of its position?

## Evidence

Read eligible issuer business and risk disclosures, comparable peer filings,
and applicable dated industry or regulatory evidence. Identify market scope,
geography, periods and the original definitions used by every source.

## Analysis

Separate common demand, cycle, regulation, substitutes and distribution shifts
from issuer-specific share, pricing or execution changes. Select peers based on
revenue mix, geography and business model. Note differences in scale,
accounting or business exposure before comparing growth, margins or market
share. Use ranges or qualitative comparisons where reliable denominators or
comparables are missing.

Test claims about competitive durability against peer outcomes, customer
behavior or documented barriers to entry. Compare against operating and
financial findings when the same driver is implicated. Keep issuer estimates
distinct from independent corroboration.

## Completion

Explain the peer set and comparison limits, the best evidence for and against
the claimed position, and implications for the assumptions used downstream.
Keep required conclusions pending when comparability or corroboration is
insufficient.
