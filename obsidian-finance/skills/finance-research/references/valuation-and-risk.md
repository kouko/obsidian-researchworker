# Valuation and risk

Apply the shared evidence and time rules in [research-method.md](research-method.md).

## Research questions

What valuation follows from supported assumptions? Which risks change the
result, its timing or the security holder's ability to realize the outcome?

## Evidence

Use eligible, verified financial, operating and industry results wherever they
support material assumptions. Retain dated market price, shares, currency,
rates, benchmarks and other valuation inputs when consumed. Trace any adopted
forecast or model to its actual applied input identity and check its current
content.

## Analysis

Name the model, its suitability and its limits. Separate operating forecasts,
intrinsic valuation and comparisons against the observed market price. Show
reproducible calculations and sensitivity to key assumptions. A next-quarter
earnings bridge needs independent support before it can inform terminal growth,
a valuation multiple or a benchmark return.

Record risks as evidence emerges and test their channels, triggers and effects
in the scenarios. Match methods to the industry and security. For banks,
equity, dividend or excess-return models require adequate inputs. Deposit
protection and transfers of bank assets do not establish listed-parent equity
recovery. For energy, test cycle and maintenance-investment assumptions. One
profitable quarter does not establish mid-cycle earnings, and negative earnings
do not yield a meaningful positive trailing P/E.

## Completion

Document ranges, scenario assumptions, materially adverse evidence, and
invalidation triggers. When market price changes, refresh every valuation
result that consumed that price directly or through a model input, such as
market-value capital weights. Reuse valid intrinsic-value work independent of
the changed price. Pass revised results to affected consumers, including the
technical price-versus-value comparison and synthesis. Without the required
model evidence or eligible quote, leave the affected conclusion pending and
identify what can still be supported.
