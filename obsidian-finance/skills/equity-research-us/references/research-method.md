# Evidence-bound research method

Read before substantive analysis or completion decisions. This is generic
methodology; private assumptions and the applied method identity belong in the
selected research root. Use the package README for exact version-1 field shapes.
No fixed number of notes or formulas substitutes for a researched answer.

## Evidence and time identities

For every material claim retain the original reference, publication time,
observation period, fiscal identity where applicable, raw bytes/hash and the
actual passage/table/cell used. A retrieval date does not establish historical
availability. Retain issuer/accession, form, official filing date and source
URL; verify amendments and original versus restated definitions. Companyfacts
labels nominate evidence; original filing declarations bind fiscal identity.

Use only economic information eligible at the assessment cutoff. A later
filing's prior-year comparative row is not evidence that the row was available
at the earlier cutoff. Keep unavailable/future rows visibly quarantined. Current
endpoint caches and a few long observation spans do not establish twenty years
of original-vintage quarterly coverage. Enumerate the expected source-declared
quarters and inspect original evidence before making that coverage claim.

Market price, currency, rates, industry studies and macro inputs need their own
observation/publication dates and units. A day cutoff does not prove intraday
tradability. A 2026 reconstruction can use a genuinely 2026-authored method or
assumption while economic inputs remain limited to 2024; label that authorship
explicitly, never backdate it or classify a future economic fact as an assumption.
Historical reconstruction is not a blind forecast or a contemporaneous report.

Store a stable logical source ID across revisions of the same economic input;
give a new financial quarter its own fiscal-bound ID. Retain old revisions and
raw originals. Compare relevant projections with JSON types and units intact:
`true`, `1` and `"1"` differ. A raw hash supports provenance, not semantic
equivalence. JSON formatting/mtime alone does not change an analysis, whereas
free-text revisions cannot be dismissed as formatting without verifying their
meaning; the text-source contract retains the full original in `raw_text`.

## Original reading coverage

Maintain a compact claim-to-source/read-coverage record in the analysis. Identify
sections and passage/table references actually examined, the dimensions they
support, unread exclusions and the effect on conclusions. Downloading a complete
HTML document, extracting declarations or checking three tables is not reading
the entire filing. Selected excerpts can support a bounded question, never a
claim of complete investment diligence. Do not claim unseen sections were read.

Read original narrative alongside numbers: business and competition; MD&A's
drivers and reconciliations; financial statement notes for accounting, segments,
commitments and contingencies; risk factors; capital allocation and the relevant
governance/proxy disclosures. Select coverage according to the claim, not an
invented mandatory word-for-word reading rule. If necessary passages are absent,
acquire them when authorized or narrow the conclusion and leave required work
pending. Issuer press releases and companyfacts are useful entry evidence, not
substitutes for original passages supporting the claimed dimensions.

## Execute substantive questions

Follow the folder's configured questions and dependency graph. For each affected
question state the question, eligible evidence, reasoning, strongest competing
explanation, missing evidence and what would change the answer. Preserve valid
independent answers. Recompute dependent synthesis when evidence, assumptions,
method versions or declared question dependencies change. Do not reduce required
questions or rebind old outputs to new dependencies to create apparent completion.

Independently reusable answers need their own complete input mapping. Trace each
material claim, arithmetic operand and competing explanation to its economic
source or authored analysis input. Earlier-period values used in a difference
and industry evidence used as counterevidence are dependencies of the answer
that uses them, even if another answer already records them. Do not require
unrelated quarters or assumptions simply to make the mapping look exhaustive.

Adopting another study's model, interpretation or completion judgment is also an
input choice. Preserve its actual authorship and the exact adopted content in a
local applied-method identity; this does not turn its economic observations into
methods or make later facts historically eligible. A compact manifest may name
the necessary adopted files or selected projections, but compare each reference
to the actual current content before relying on reuse. Preserve and investigate
mismatches, then version the applied identity and reassess its consumers. An
unchecked list of hashes or hashes hidden in self-checks cannot detect change.
A self-contained adopted snapshot is another option when the analysis genuinely
uses that bound snapshot rather than live references. Neither option requires
recursive certification of every source behind an already accepted finite study.

Apply the appropriate framework for each affected question. Keep broad coverage
where the configured question needs it and avoid duplicating evidence across
notes. The same note may cover several questions, and a question may draw on
several frameworks. Use the frameworks as guidance for the actual question
scope, with output paths and coverage governed by the existing review contract.

The bundled frameworks cover individual companies. ETF, commodity and FX
judgments need their own applicable methods and evidence. For an industry
contrast, consult the repository's `docs/finance/pilots/industry-contrasts-v1`
when available. Those examples illustrate how methods differ by industry.
They do not establish accepted company research, calibrated models or expected
excess returns.

## Completion and continuation

Check sources, citations, calculations and counterevidence against the actual
output before writing its verification record. Correct arithmetic and matching
hashes do not establish source coverage or substantive quality. Record author
self-checks honestly; do not label them independent review. Unresolved required
analysis remains pending even when a partial research envelope can be saved.

When sufficient evidence exists for an independent scoped unit, persist that
unit's analysis plus its real economic dependencies and local method/assumption
identities in a managed partial checkpoint, keeping blocked dimensions pending.
This lets the next invocation reuse actual progress rather than rediscovering a
standalone partial note. If an entire question still lacks necessary coverage,
retain its partial findings without declaring that question complete. Explain
that distinction explicitly. Complete publication requires all required questions
and gaps resolved; structural validation cannot make that decision for the agent.

Retain original source hashes, exact relevant projections, fiscal coverage,
cutoff, actual method/assumption authorship, horizon convention, changes,
predecessor link and unresolved gaps in the review. Keep generic methodology in
the bundled references; an applied-method manifest needs a content/version identity and
selected conventions, not a copied manual. Preserve all reports and user edits.
Source-cache audits, successful helpers and industry examples leave their stated
runtime and research limitations intact. Installed-host continuation, actual
external freshness, analytical sufficiency and program completion each require
their own observed evidence.
