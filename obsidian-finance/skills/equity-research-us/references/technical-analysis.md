# Technical analysis

Apply the shared evidence and time rules in [research-method.md](research-method.md).

## Minimum output

State the eligible price and volume date range, exchange and split adjustment basis. Give evidence and limits for the material trend, levels and invalidation signal. Where a consistent data series is unavailable, specify the missing observations and which signals cannot be assessed. Distinguish price action from comparison against independently established value.

## Research questions

What do the eligible trading data show about trend, momentum, trading activity
or volatility over the selected window? Where does the observed price stand
relative to the applicable estimated value range?

## Evidence

Use an eligible dated price series and volume where required by the metric.
Record the instrument, listing, currency, trading session and timezone, data
frequency, observation and availability times, adjustment basis, and coverage.
Retain benchmark data if used. Keep all consumed observations within the
assessment cutoff, including any information used for corporate-action
adjustments. Do not treat a partial bar as a completed session.

For the price-versus-value comparison use an eligible range from
[valuation and risk](valuation-and-risk.md). Record the quote and valuation
dates, comparable currency and per-share basis, and the valuation inputs
actually adopted. Keep the analysis cutoff explicit.

## Analysis

Choose only metrics relevant to the question. State the window and calculation
for returns, trend, relative performance or volatility. Align benchmarks and
corporate-action treatment before comparing returns. Distinguish raw closing
prices and adjusted prices, and price returns and total returns. When price and
volume disagree or observations are incomplete, explain the confidence limit
and avoid projecting a technical result into business fundamentals.

Report the price-versus-value comparison separately from price and volume
signals. Compare the observed price with the estimated range on a common basis
and show any conversions. Present market-derived support or resistance and
the valuation range as distinct results with separate evidence. If the range
or a compatible price is unavailable, record the gap and report independently
supported market observations.

## Completion

Bind material observations and calculations to their sources. State the
supported observation, counterevidence and the next observable change that
would invalidate the interpretation. Recalculate affected metrics when price
or volume changes. When the quote changes, refresh the price-versus-value
comparison with the updated eligible quote and applicable valuation range,
including a reused range that remains valid. When only the adopted valuation
changes, refresh the comparison using the still-eligible quote and preserve the
still-valid market signals.
Leave the affected in-scope work pending if its inputs or sufficient lookback
are unavailable, and tell synthesis which conclusions the gap limits.
