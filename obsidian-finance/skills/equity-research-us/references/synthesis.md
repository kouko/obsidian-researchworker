# Synthesis

Apply the shared evidence and time rules in [research-method.md](research-method.md).

## Minimum output

Summarize the decision-relevant changes from the prior quarter, with the strongest support and counterevidence linked to the source analyses. For each horizon state the outlook and investment recommendation explicitly, alongside the evidence and reassessment conditions. Rank outstanding gaps by their effect on the judgment and specify which evidence would resolve them.

## Present quantitative evidence in tables

Use the template tables for material changes and thesis checks. Give each
quantitative comparison its own row when the measures or periods differ. Put
the period, unit, comparison basis, and source next to the figure. Label
reported values, recalculations, and model assumptions in the cells. Place
counterevidence on the same row as the thesis it challenges. Put material
scenario results and numeric triggers elsewhere in compact supporting tables.
Use the conclusion and surrounding prose for interpretation.

## Research questions

Which conditional investment judgments follow from the applicable evidence at
each horizon? What changed since the predecessor, and what observations could
reverse the thesis?

## Evidence

Use applicable findings from the other frameworks after checking their cutoff,
read coverage and bound inputs. Reuse still-valid results for unaffected
questions. Include conflicting findings and unresolved work, allowing a
limited conclusion where an independent line of evidence permits it.
Distinguish technical observations about trading from claims about business
performance and value. Use the adopted [forward forecast](forward-forecast.md)
where it supports the judgment and distinguish its scenarios from management
guidance, analyst consensus and price-implied expectations.

## Analysis

Test the preferred thesis against contrary evidence, unresolved drivers and
alternative causal explanations. Explain changes across quarters and differences
in definitions or acquired business scope. Summarize material forecast revisions
and their reasons, and which horizon judgments depend on changed assumptions
or remaining gaps. Present realized forecast errors only for verified frozen
pre-release versions that meet the forecast reference's scoring rules. Label
any reconstructed comparisons separately.
For disagreement among findings, show which assumptions, horizon or evidence
quality explain the difference and which judgment is affected. A missing technical input can constrain a
price-sensitive short-term judgment while independent operating conclusions
remain supported. Propagate a price revision only to judgments that consume
the changed observation or a result that depends on it.

## Conditional horizons

Separate analysis windows from the user's unknown holding preference. Preserve
an established method's durations and state their anchor date and boundaries.
The initial pilot's short 0–3, medium 3–12 and long 12–60 months differs from
the later exploratory one-quarter / 1–3-year / 5+-year convention. Name the
selected convention and this difference explicitly; never silently merge the
two, use one quarter to support a five-year forecast or present either as a
user-confirmed holding period. A changed convention is an authored method
change with affected comparisons, not a cosmetic label edit. Where no window
is established, identify the proposed analysis windows and any decision-relevant
ambiguity rather than inventing the user's preference.

Fill one subsection per horizon in the synthesis template. Put the outlook
and recommendation in the compact table before the supporting detail. Keep
long reasoning below the outlook table and enter triggers in the reassessment
table. Every subsection must state:

- **Dates.** Give the date range, tied to the review cutoff or the documented
  convention. Identify any historical price by its observation date.
- **Outlook.** State the supported price or return direction in plain language,
  such as upward, downward, range-bound, or undetermined. Name the subject
  explicitly. Where only the operating direction is supported, state it
  separately and mark the price or return outlook as undetermined. Label
  modeled directions with their assumptions.
- **Investment recommendation.** State an action separately from the outlook.
  Give a conditional recommendation when the evidence supports it. Otherwise,
  say to defer the investment decision, name the missing evidence, and say
  when to reassess. A deferred decision is distinct from a hold rating.
- **Basis and counterevidence.** Cite the relevant report or evidence next to
  the claim. Preserve the date, units and scope of any material number.
- **Reassessment triggers.** Add a table row per observable trigger, with the
  full condition, the change to the outlook or action, and a link to the
  supporting analysis. Keep combined clauses in the same cell and preserve
  conjunctions, thresholds, units, time windows, price type and volume
  requirements. If a trigger remains unverified, record the missing evidence
  and the action it prevents in its own row. Keep quantitative trigger levels
  in the table when writing the surrounding explanation.

Use research language in the report. Reserve `insufficient_evidence` for the
existing machine record. Do not invent scenario probabilities, benchmark
returns, price targets or precision. Evidence supporting an operating outlook
alone cannot support an investment rating.

## Investment recommendation check

Adapt the research-content checks in [FINRA Rule 2241(c)](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2241)
for internal review. Before stating a recommendation, rating or target, show its
evidence, valuation method and dated inputs, the applicable horizon, any
benchmark used, and material downside risks and observable invalidation
conditions. Define any rating used. Present the strongest material downside
evidence alongside the case for the judgment.

When a recommendation, rating or target lacks a defensible basis, leave it
pending and give the evidence needed to support it. A recalculable sensitivity
table establishes how assumptions change the output. A target also requires
evidence for the selected inputs and method.

## Completion

Record all three horizon judgments in the existing review format, with
evidence and invalidation triggers. Include changes from the predecessor and
the impact of missing evidence. Bind adopted analysis and material economic
inputs under the shared method. Apply its completion criteria to required
questions and keep unresolved work pending even if partial synthesis is useful.
