# Operating analysis

Apply the shared evidence and time rules in [research-method.md](research-method.md).

## Minimum output

Show the material operating drivers and their direction versus a comparable period. Separate management's explanation from quantified contributions and unsupported hypotheses. Connect at least one change to a durability check using an observable metric. Missing volume, price, mix, or currency attribution should remain explicitly unresolved rather than turning a residual into an estimate of one driver.

## Research questions

How does the company earn and reinvest money? Which products, segments,
customers or operating decisions explain the change, and which changes could
persist?

## Evidence

Use business descriptions, management discussion, segment and cash flow notes,
and relevant governance or proxy disclosures. Check investment, distributions,
dilution, incentives, control and related-party issues where material.

## Analysis

Separate demand, volumes, price, mix and currency effects. Compare aligned
segment and period definitions, tracing changes in scope to acquisitions or
reporting changes. Describe customer concentration and distribution where they
affect operating performance. Mark unobserved drivers as hypotheses. Missing
segment weights prevent combining unlike growth rates.

Choose metrics for the business. For subscriptions and membership retail, check
renewals, investment and the economics of an individual customer or location.
For energy, separate volumes, realized prices, cycle effects and investment
needed to sustain production. Connect capital allocation to operating outcomes.
Assess the cost and per-share effect of buybacks alongside dilution and other
uses of funds before drawing conclusions about value creation or alignment.

## Completion

Explain the supported operating drivers, competing explanations, and missing
comparisons. Cite original disclosures, reconcile material drivers with
financial results where possible, and identify the uncertainty that should
carry into valuation or synthesis.
