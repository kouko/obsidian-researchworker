# Finding U.S. earnings-call evidence

Look up the call when management remarks, guidance, or analyst Q&A could
materially affect operating, forecast, or risk analysis. Use the issuer's fiscal
quarter and the call date from the earnings release. Recent and historical calls
have different coverage. Search manually for the needed quarter and keep any
unavailable call evidence as a gap.

For a recent quarter, check the issuer's event page and SEC attachments while
the replay is still likely to be available. For older quarters, use the issuer
archive and SEC filing index, then try a transcript index, a historical text
dataset, or contemporaneous coverage. Stop once you have enough traceable
evidence for the question.

| Source | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Company investor relations | Start in the quarterly results or events archive. Look for a transcript, recording, prepared remarks, or CFO commentary. [Microsoft FY2024 Q3](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2024/earnings-fy-2024-q3) and [Alphabet 2024 Q3](https://abc.xyz/investor/events/event-details/2024/2024-Q3-Earnings-Call/) have published transcripts. [NVIDIA's financial reports](https://investor.nvidia.com/financial-info/financial-reports/default.aspx) distinguish transcripts, audio, and CFO commentary. [Apple FY2024 Q3](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/08/apple-reports-third-quarter-results/) announced about two weeks of replay. Check the issuer's official video or podcast channel when the event page lacks a working replay. |
| SEC EDGAR | Inspect the earnings Form 8-K and nearby amendments or later reports. Under Item 2.02, look at attachment descriptions for transcript, prepared remarks, or presentation. [Apple FY2024 Q3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000080/0000320193-24-000080-index.htm) has a release with replay details. [Where Food Comes From](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1360565/000149315226051832/form8-k.htm) includes a transcript. Verify the attachment content and its public availability time. |
| Stock Analysis and Quartr | [Apple's Stock Analysis index](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/transcripts/) lists recent and historical calls. Public text was visible for [FY2026 Q4](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/transcripts/702819-q4-2026/) and [FY2011 Q2](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/transcripts/63347-q2-2011/). The pages offer audio playback, which must be checked separately. [Quartr's free mobile app](https://quartr.com/products/mobile-app) offers human access to calls and text. Treat these as lookup options and respect [the site's access terms](https://stockanalysis.com/terms-of-use/). |
| Publisher archives | Search by ticker, fiscal quarter, and confirmed call date plus `earnings call transcript` or `conference call notes`. [The Motley Fool's Apple FY2019 Q3 page](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2019/07/30/apple-inc-aapl-q3-2019-earnings-call-transcript.aspx) contains remarks and Q&A. [AppleInsider's FY2006 Q3 notes](https://appleinsider.com/articles/06/07/19/notes_from_apples_q306_financial_conference_call) are a historical summary. News can support specific attributed claims when a full transcript cannot be found. |
| Historical bulk text | [Kurry S&P 500 Earnings Transcripts](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kurry/sp500_earnings_transcripts) lists text records from 2005 to part of 2025. Check issuer and quarter coverage, completeness, and upstream provenance. Its card shows MIT metadata and research/education language. Clarify usage rights before retaining or redistributing bulk data. |

When text is unavailable and a recording can be saved within its access and
usage terms, local speech-to-text is an option. Label the output as machine
transcribed, keep timecodes, and verify material numbers, units, names, and
guidance against the recording or filing. A playback link alone does not
establish that an audio file is downloadable.

When adopting evidence, use the existing quarterly source registration. Record
the issuer, fiscal quarter, call time, source URL, verified public availability,
and whether it is a full transcript, excerpt, prepared remarks, or summary.
Keep retrieval and transcription dates separate from historical availability.
Locate each material claim by speaker and section or timecode. Distinguish
management guidance, analyst questions, and realized results. Attribute a
summary's claims to its publisher when the speaker or passage cannot be checked.
Exclude information whose public availability by the cutoff cannot be verified.
Keep call evidence out of mandatory source lists. Check an adopted call source
before reusing analysis that relies on it. If the source can no longer be
verified, recheck the affected conclusions and continue independently supported
work.

Use formal financial statements or SEC filings available by the cutoff for
reported financial results. Before deciding that figures conflict, match the
financial metric, reporting period, accounting basis (such as GAAP or adjusted),
currency, and units. If comparable figures disagree, cite the formal figure
and flag the discrepancy. Exclude later amendments from earlier assessments.
Label call-only figures as management commentary or guidance, state what was
verified, and keep them separate from reported financial results. An analyst's
estimate in a question remains an analyst estimate unless management confirms it.
