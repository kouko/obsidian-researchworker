# Global semiconductors | 2024 Q2–Q3 outlook review

Track worldwide semiconductor sales in nominal US dollars. Use the quarterly
totals as reported. SIA's separately published monthly series is a three-month
moving average. Its regional monthly commentary cannot substitute for quarterly
vendor shares, unit volumes or product mix.

## Q2 baseline, August 5 information cutoff

SIA reported $149.9 billion, up 18.3% year over year and 6.5% from Q1.
The contemporaneous WSTS spring outlook projected $611 billion for calendar
2024 (+16.0%), led by memory and logic. This was an annual outlook, so growth
in Q3 can test the direction of recovery but cannot score annual forecast error.

- [SIA Q2 release](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-18-3-in-q2-2024-compared-to-q2-2023-quarter-to-quarter-sales-up-6-5/)
- [WSTS spring forecast](https://www.wsts.org/76/103/WSTS-Semiconductor-Market-Forecast-Spring-2024)

## Q3 update, November 5 information cutoff

SIA reported $166.0 billion, up 23.2% year over year and 10.7% from Q2.
Rounded original releases imply $16.1 billion sequential growth, or 10.74%.
Q3 was consistent with the anticipated rebound, with annual growth accelerating
4.9 percentage points. The releases alone cannot attribute that acceleration
to memory and logic. Carry the spring outlook into the Q3 record as the
eligible published forecast. Watch Q4 growth in the reported industry
sales series and product-category contributions. Leave annual forecast
scoring pending until the annual actual is available.

- [SIA Q3 release](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-23-2-in-q3-2024-compared-to-q3-2023-quarter-to-quarter-sales-up-10-7/)

## Later evidence, excluded from the two snapshots

On December 3, WSTS updated the annual growth forecast to 19.0% and raised
expected memory growth from 76.8% to 81.0% and logic from 10.7% to 16.9%.
The annual actual published February 7 was $627.6 billion (+19.1%).
Relative to the spring outlook, actual growth was 3.1 percentage points
higher. On rounded headline amounts actual sales exceeded the forecast by
approximately $16.6 billion. This retrospective evaluation belongs in a
later record, with links back to both forecast versions.

- [WSTS fall forecast](https://www.wsts.org/esraCMS/extension/media/f/WST/6765/WSTS_FC-Release-2024_11.pdf)
- [SIA annual actual](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-19-1-in-2024-double-digit-growth-projected-in-2025/)

## Design implication

Record the target horizon separately from the quarter in which an outlook is
saved. Preserve baseline and revisions separately. Compare against the actual
only after the target period is reported. A quarter can review interim
direction while leaving final scoring pending.
