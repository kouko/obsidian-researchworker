# EU passenger cars | Reconstructing calendar Q3 2024

Use the EU row in each ACEA first monthly release. Count new passenger car
registrations and battery-electric (BEV) registrations. Exclude plug-in
hybrids from BEV and exclude UK and EFTA from the denominator.

| Month | 2024 BEV | 2024 total | 2023 BEV | 2023 total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [July](https://www.acea.auto/files/Press_release_car_registrations_July_2024.pdf) | 102,705 | 852,051 | 115,100 | 850,068 |
| [August](https://www.acea.auto/files/Press_release_car_registrations_August-2024.pdf) | 92,627 | 643,637 | 165,204 | 787,812 |
| [September](https://www.acea.auto/files/Press_release_car_registrations_September_2024.pdf) | 139,702 | 809,163 | 127,196 | 861,973 |
| Quarter | 335,034 | 2,304,851 | 407,500 | 2,499,853 |

Quarter shares are the sum of BEV registrations divided by the sum of all
registrations, 14.54% versus 16.30% a year earlier. BEV volume dropped 17.78%
year over year. September alone had positive year-on-year BEV growth, so it
would give a different directional reading from the full quarter.

September's first release was scheduled for 04:00 UTC on October 22.
Before that release the snapshots used here could only support a Q3
partial result. The prior-year comparison uses the year-earlier columns
published in the same three 2024 releases. These are not proof of what
a researcher knew in 2023. ACEA distinguishes provisional monthly releases
from consolidated figures. A later consolidated quarter would be a distinct
input set.

For the skill, a derived quarter needs a component list, a completeness
check, a recorded formula, and a citation to each release. Ratios require
recomputing from their components rather than averaging rounded monthly
shares. This case only tests aggregation and cutoff, with no separate
forecast or successive-quarter skill run.
