# Industry and market research methods for investment research

## Question and scope

What workflow and completion criteria should an independent market study use
if its findings must be updated over time and cited by issuer research?
The smartphone case tests source selection and comparability on public
releases. It is not a full market study or a test of cross-session storage.

## Method and rationale

Compare original publications from investment education, competition
authorities, business-entry guidance and commercial market tracking. Check the
proposed workflow against the existing
[issuer research skill](../../../../obsidian-finance/skills/finance-research/SKILL.md),
[issuer evidence rules](../../../../obsidian-finance/skills/finance-research/references/research-method.md)
and [industry and competition guide](../../../../obsidian-finance/skills/finance-research/references/industry-and-competition.md).

## Findings and evidence

- [CFA Institute, 2026 equity curriculum](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/industry-and-competitive-analysis):
  define the industry, estimate size, growth, profitability and shares, then
  analyze industry structure and external forces. Examine rivalry, entry,
  substitutes and buyer/supplier bargaining power when material. Company
  positioning is a downstream application.
- [US DOJ market definition](https://www.justice.gov/atr/merger-guidelines/tools/market-definition)
  and [OECD market studies overview](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/market-studies-and-other-market-analysis-tools-for-competition-authorities_66ed0da7-en/full-report/component-4.html):
  product and geographic boundaries and buyer switching sharpen the study.
  Antitrust enforcement tests serve a different decision and are not
  completion rules for investment research.
- [JICA market-sizing guidance](https://www.jica.go.jp/activities/schemes/priv_partner/platform/column08_20240606.html):
  direct statistics and proxy estimates need different treatment. The example
  distinguishes tractor imports from the domestic market and adds other evidence
  before estimating market size. For surveys specifically,
  [AAPOR disclosure standards](https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/disclosure-standards/)
  cover population, samples, collection, weighting and limitations.
- [IDC calendar Q2 2024](https://www.idc.com/resource-center/press-releases/worldwide-smartphone-market-grew-6-5-in-the-second-quarter-of-2024-as-momentum-continues-to-build-according-to-idc-tracker/)
  and [calendar Q3 2024](https://www.idc.com/resource-center/press-releases/worldwide-smartphone-market-grows-4-with-316-1-million-units-shipped-in-the-third-quarter-of-2024-according-to-idc/):
  dated preliminary tables, same tracker and stated coverage, 285.4m and 316.1m
  worldwide shipments, excluding refurbished units. Reported year-over-year
  growth was 6.5% and 4.0%. The Q2 page header says July 14 while the body and
  table say July 15. Use July 15 conservatively at day granularity without
  asserting an exact release timestamp. Q3 table is dated October 14.
- [Counterpoint October 15](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/global-smartphone-market-q3-2024)
  measured Q3 sales to end users, +2% year over year. Its
  [November 1 release](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/q3-2024-global-smartphone-shipments-grow-2-revenues-and-asp-reach-highest-ever-for-a-third-quarter)
  estimated 307m shipments. It is a separate shipment estimate from IDC's
  316.1m. The [UK CMA mobile ecosystems appendix, C, paragraphs 21–23](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/1052590/Appendices_and_Glossary___.pdf)
  documents the inventory and timing distinction and notes that provider
  estimates can differ by definition, source, geography and time capture.
  The public releases do not establish the contribution of each factor to
  the 9.1m gap. Do not average the series or infer inventory from their gap.

## Decision impact

Recommend a shared evidence and continuation method with a market-specific
workflow. Define product, geography, time, denominator and exclusions.
Select a consistent source series and distinguish observation time, first
public availability and revision. Measure size, growth, mix and shares where
supported. Test explanations using material demand, supply, channel, cost and
competitive evidence. Keep forecasts conditional on explicit assumptions
and indicators, and distinguish observed results, hypotheses and outlook.
Save eligible claims with exact sources, unresolved questions and triggers
for reassessment. A company study can reference a specific market finding
and its source version while making its own issuer-specific judgment.

A smaller first pass uses one market, one comparable two-period series and
one issuer citation. A standalone one-off market memo can answer a current
question but does not exercise continuity. Extending issuer-only analysis
preserves current usage but does not demonstrate a shared market record.
A full market study adds structural and forward analysis where evidence
supports it. Treat those questions as open if the first two releases cannot
answer them.

## Limitations and open questions

This synthesis describes the inspected public practices, not a universal
industry standard or a tracker methodology audit. Two adjacent periods
can verify comparison and updating, but do not establish a long-term trend
or identify causes. Calendar market quarters, Apple fiscal quarters and
assessment cutoffs each need independent matching. Shared storage and
the Apple citation path have not been implemented or tested here.
