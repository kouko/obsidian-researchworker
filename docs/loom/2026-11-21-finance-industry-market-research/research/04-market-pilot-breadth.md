# How many markets to study before creating the skill

## Question and approach

Compare one in-depth market, several in-depth markets, and one in-depth
market plus a narrow contrasting replay. The decision is whether a second
sample exposes a different evidence contract at manageable scope.
Use the same calendar quarters to keep differences in measurement visible.

## Findings

The [smartphone pilot](03-smartphone-quarterly-pilot.md) tests early
estimates, shipment versus end-user sales, market versus issuer timing,
and the gap between unit share and company revenue.

A narrow global semiconductor replay used the SIA releases based on
WSTS statistics. Calendar Q2 2024 sales were $149.9 billion (+18.3% year
over year), published August 5. Q3 sales reached $166.0 billion (+23.2%
year over year), published November 5. The rounded values imply +10.74%
quarter over quarter, consistent with SIA's +10.7%. Q3 is unavailable at
the pilot's October 31 cutoff. The releases provide quarterly totals
and report monthly metrics as three-month moving averages. Use the
quarterly total as published and avoid treating adjacent rolling averages
as independent monthly measurements. The release's regional growth
commentary does not establish quarterly vendor shares or which end
market caused total growth.

[SIA Q2 release](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-18-3-in-q2-2024-compared-to-q2-2023-quarter-to-quarter-sales-up-6-5/)
· [SIA Q3 release](https://www.semiconductors.org/global-semiconductor-sales-increase-23-2-in-q3-2024-compared-to-q3-2023-quarter-to-quarter-sales-up-10-7/)

## Decision impact

Recommend one deep case plus one narrow contrasting replay before
skill authoring. The replay already exposes three differences: revenue
rather than unit shipments, regional metrics without observable vendor
shares in the release, and quarterly totals alongside monthly rolling
averages. The common requirements are market scope, measurement unit
and aggregation method, observation period and public date, original
source, comparable change, explanation limits and next checks. Vendor
share, shipment volume and regional split should be selected where
material and supported, rather than universally required.

A single full smartphone case covers the Apple handoff but misses these
differences. Multiple full studies would add structural and forecast
research before a specific unresolved question justifies the cost.
A third market is warranted if a material requirement remains untested,
such as constructing a quarter from monthly observations or combining
registrations across geographies. The semiconductor replay is related
to smartphone supply chains, so it does not establish generality across
industries. It tests the evidence shape, without claiming full sector
coverage, runtime continuity or installed skill behavior.
