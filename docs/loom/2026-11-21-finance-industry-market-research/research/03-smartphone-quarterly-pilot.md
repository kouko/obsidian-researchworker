# Global smartphone market | 2024 calendar Q2–Q3 pilot

## Scope and evidence

Track new worldwide smartphone shipments and company shipment shares.
IDC counts branded shipments by parent company and excludes refurbished
units and OEM sales. The Q2 table is dated July 15, 2024 (the page banner
shows July 14; use July 15 conservatively), and the Q3 table October 14.
Both are preliminary estimates; retain each dated release for the replay.
This is a retrospective analysis using evidence available by the end of
October 31, 2024 in Pacific time, not a contemporaneously authored report.

Sequential volume changes use (Q3 / Q2 − 1) on rounded table inputs.
Year-over-year volume changes use IDC's reported changes. Share changes
subtract reported shares. The Q2 market grew 6.5% year over year and Q3
grew 4.0%, so volumes rose sequentially while annual growth slowed.
The two reported growth rates compare with their own prior-year bases.

Apple's sequential share gain did not persist as a gain against the same
season a year earlier. Vivo gained 1.3 points year over year while Samsung
lost 1.3, so the recovery did not translate into uniform gains among
leading companies. Q2's vivo/OPPO difference is an IDC statistical tie.

## Explanations and evidence limits

IDC attributes Apple’s shipment growth to older-model demand and iPhone
16's launch. Counterpoint estimates Apple’s Q3 sales to end users were
roughly flat year over year even though it led in September. These
observations measure different transactions. Timing, inventory and tracker
coverage are plausible explanations for the gap. The two reports do not
isolate their relative effects or prove channel stocking. IDC's Q2 release
also flags a low comparison base, so the change in annual growth alone
cannot establish weaker underlying demand.

## Forecast carry-forward

At the July 15 Q2 baseline, the eligible IDC annual outlook was its May 30
forecast of 1.21 billion shipments (+4.0%). At the October 31 Q3 cutoff, carry
that forecast forward as the prior version and record the August 27 revision
to 1.23 billion (+5.8%), up 1.8 percentage points. IDC attributed the revision
to stronger Android growth in China and emerging markets. Its August release
describes Q2 growth as 9%, while the July initial Q2 table says 6.5%. Record
the discrepancy and retain the July value for the original quarterly record.
The August commentary alone does not provide a reconciled Q2 table.

Q3's initial +4.0% year-over-year results were directionally consistent with
full-year growth. Leave the annual forecast review pending at the October
cutoff. For Q4, check whether holiday-quarter shipments remain above the
prior year and whether Android demand corroborates the stated driver.
Later, IDC's January 13 release put preliminary annual growth at +6.4%,
0.6 percentage points above the August forecast and 2.4 above the May
forecast. That comparison belongs in a later review. Keep the January
estimate labeled preliminary.
