work for program Stage A. This is research and sample-note authoring, not a new

## Work and completion criteria

1. Fix the research purpose, fiscal-quarter identity, assessment cutoff and
   source-vintage rules; keep financial quarters separate from refresh cadence.
2. Inspect primary professional-method/report references and select a small
   common core; distinguish external guidance from local proposed conventions.
3. Acquire and reconcile Apple 2024Q2/2024Q3 financial evidence, including
   annual/YTD/discrete periods, issuer narrative and material coverage gaps.
4. Draft dimension and synthesis notes for both quarters, plus a cross-period
   comparison; preserve missing-data and insufficient-valuation outcomes.
5. Verify calculations, citations, links and temporal exclusions; record Stage A
   evidence and the unresolved decisions that block later full-case claims.

Use only public sources. No real vault writes, global installation, paid tools,
background execution, publication or merge. Existing accepted acquisition code
is unchanged. Large raw downloads remain outside Git; retain the compact values,
provenance and calculations required to review the sample in this directory.
