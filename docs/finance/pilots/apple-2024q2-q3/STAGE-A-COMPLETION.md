## What the pilot establishes

The source-based Q2 and Q3 narratives differ where financial/operating evidence
differs; a shared quarter label does not freeze market updates. The selected
evidence is reviewable without depending on an original Monkey Skills runtime,
private cache or an installed finance plugin. Generic method research remains
in the repository; no duplicate policy manual was written to the vault.

The samples deliberately distinguish fundamental interpretation from a security
recommendation. Both have valuation evidence gaps and therefore produce an
insufficient-evidence result for each proposed horizon rather than a fabricated
buy/sell/hold or target. Their note states stay draft/partial. Stage A's report
examples can be complete as examples while the underlying investment case is
explicitly incomplete.
