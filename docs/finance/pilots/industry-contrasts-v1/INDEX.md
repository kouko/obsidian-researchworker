# Industry contrast research v1

Status: in progress, evidence-bound method contrasts; not full Stage D acceptance.
Authored 2026-11-16. Public originals only; historical reconstruction, not a
blind forecast, trade recommendation or contemporary publication. Runtime code,
accepted attestations, original Apple notes and the real vault are unchanged.

## Scope and gate

Use JPMorgan 2020Q1/2024Q2 and ExxonMobil 2020Q2/2024Q2 to test credit stress,
one-off bank earnings, commodity losses, acquisition scope and cash-flow
definitions. The 2024 cases are comparison periods, not asserted universally
normal or representative industry averages. The 2020 notes cannot use 2024
outcomes. The cross-period method discussion is authored in 2026 and may compare
both; do not copy that hindsight into a historical judgment.

Read [bank cases](銀行案例.md), [energy cases](能源案例.md), and the selected
[inputs/calculations](evidence.json). Figures are USD million unless explicitly
labeled. Rounded headline values stay rounded; ratios are not silently combined
across GAAP/non-GAAP, calendar vintages or regulatory capital definitions.

## Method transfer decisions

- Banks: credit provisions, funding/deposits and capital constrain distributions.
  Do not apply an industrial company's debt-subtracted enterprise valuation or
  CFO-minus-capex proxy mechanically. NYU's original financial-service valuation
  discussion explains why debt and reinvestment are difficult to define for these
  firms. An equity-oriented dividend/excess-return approach is a candidate, not
  a completed valuation or a universal bank formula.
- Energy: separate volumes, realizations, refining/chemical margins, investment
  needs and acquired production. A profitable quarter is not mid-cycle earnings;
  a negative quarter cannot support a meaningful positive trailing P/E. Keep the
  issuer's cash-flow definition distinct from a local CFO-minus-PP&E proxy.
- Both: general report questions remain, but their metric bodies differ. Refuse
  to manufacture missing inputs or carry Apple scenario margins into another
  industry. Record exactly which model is unsuitable and why.

Method source inspected: [Aswath Damodaran, financial-service characteristics](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/littlebook/financialsvccompanies.htm).
This is current method reference material, not a dated 2020 market input or
evidence that any model produced excess returns.

## Remaining evidence

Selected original press releases and reconciliations are not complete 10-Q
research. Original accession-bound statements, detailed credit/reserve and
funding structure, commodity-price/realization histories, sustaining investment,
peer/price/expectation evidence and independent analytical review remain.
Microsoft/Costco and a failed/acquired/delisted contrast also remain in the
program's proposed broader validation set. Short/medium/long investment ratings
stay insufficient evidence; mechanical arithmetic is not independent acceptance.
Do not mark D complete or launch broad twenty-year generation from this draft.
